For members

Everything you need to confess and reply

Nothing to set up. Use the buttons under the latest confession, or right-click (long-press on mobile) any confession or reply.

🔒

Anonymous, or with your name

Pick on the form. Anonymous confessions never show your name; choose “Show my name” if you want the credit.

🖼️

Pictures and videos

Attach one image or video (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, MP4, MOV or WebM, within your server's upload limit and 25 MB at most). Location, camera and capture details are removed before it's posted.

🎭

Anonymous reply threads

Every replier gets a stable alias per thread, so conversations are easy to follow without revealing anyone. Reply to a confession or to another reply.

🔔

Reply notifications

Get a DM when someone replies to your confession. Tap 🔔 after replying to also hear when someone answers you. Mute either from the DM.

📋

Your confessions, privately

See your posts, reply counts and approval status in one private list. Delete a confession, or withdraw one that's still waiting for review.

🚩

Report in two clicks

See something that crosses a line? Report it with a reason and the server's staff are notified right away.

For admins & moderators

Anonymous for members, in control for staff

Decide how confessions flow in your server and step in when something goes wrong.

🧭

Guided setup

/setup walks you through the confession channel, staff log, optional approval and locking the channel so members can only post through the bot. Then it posts the buttons.

✅

Optional approval queue

Confessions wait in a private channel for an approver role or anyone with Manage Server. Disapprove with a reason; the author gets a DM either way. Numbers are only given on approval, so there are no gaps.

📝

Staff log

Every confession, reply, deletion and report lands in your log channel, with who posted it, so you can act on abuse.

🔨

Moderation tools

Block a member from confessing and replying, either directly or by a confession's number. Delete any confession along with its reply thread.

⏸️

Pause and pace

Pause confessions and replies at any time; the buttons show “Paused by staff”. Set a per-member cooldown from 0 to 3600 seconds (replies are capped at 15 s).

🛡️

Safe by default

@everyone, role and user mentions in confessions never ping. Reports are rate-limited so the log can't be flooded. If the buttons' message is deleted, they're posted again automatically.

Get started

Set up in about a minute

You'll need Manage Server in the server you're adding it to.

  1. Add ConfessionBot

    Use the Add to Discord button, pick your server and approve the permissions.

  2. Run /setup

    Choose the confession channel and staff log, and turn on approval if you want it. You review everything before it's saved.

  3. Members press the buttons

    ConfessionBot posts Create Confession and Reply Confession in your channel. That's it.
