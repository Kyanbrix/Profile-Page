This policy explains what information Confession ("the bot", "we", "us") collects when you use it on Discord, who can see it, how long it is kept, and how you can have it removed.

By using the bot, you agree to this policy and to our Terms of Service.

The short version

  • "Anonymous" means anonymous to other members, not to server staff. Every server that uses the bot has a staff log channel. It shows the real author of every confession and reply, including anonymous ones, to anyone who can view that channel.
  • The bot stores your Discord user ID alongside what you post, so it can show you your own posts, let you delete them, and let staff enforce bans.
  • The bot does not read your ordinary messages, and it does not store the images or videos you attach.
  • A server's data is deleted 30 days after the bot is removed from that server.

What we collect

When you post a confession or reply

  • Your Discord user ID
  • The text you submit, and any content warning you add
  • Whether you chose to post anonymously or with your name shown
  • The file name and type of any attachment (not the file itself)
  • The server, channel, message and thread IDs where it was posted
  • The confession number, and when it was posted or deleted
  • For replies: which confession or reply you answered, and whether you are the confession's author

When a server uses the approval queue

  • The information above, while the confession waits for review
  • The reviewer's user ID, the decision, and any reason they give

When staff moderate

  • Confession bans: the banned user's ID, the ID of the staff member who issued the ban, the reason, and the date
  • Who deleted a confession (its author or a moderator), and who paused or resumed confessions

Notification preferences

  • Your user ID, if you turn off DMs about replies to your confessions or turn on DMs about replies to your replies

Server settings

  • The server ID; the confession, log and approval channels; the approver role; the cooldown; and when the bot joined or left

What we do not collect

  • The content of your normal messages. The bot does not have Discord's Message Content access, and it does not read or store your ordinary messages. The only message events it acts on are deletions, so it can put its buttons back if someone deletes them.
  • Your attachments. When you attach an image or video, the bot downloads it briefly and removes hidden metadata that could identify you (such as location, device model and capture time). It then uploads the file to Discord with a neutral name and deletes its temporary copy. After that, the file exists only on Discord.
  • Your anonymous alias (for example Anon #A3F). It is recalculated each time it's needed and never stored.
  • Server member lists, your profile, or your activity.

Cooldowns and rate limits are kept briefly in memory and are never written to storage.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the bot:

  • to post confessions and replies, and to give each replier a consistent alias within a thread
  • to show you your own confessions (/my-confessions) and let you delete or withdraw them
  • to send you DMs about approvals, rejections and replies
  • to let server staff review, report, delete and ban
  • to prevent spam and abuse (cooldowns, rate limits, confession bans)

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except as described below.

Server staff decide who can view the log and approval channels. Each server is responsible for how its own staff use this information.

We may also disclose information:

  • if required by law or by a valid legal request,
  • to Discord's Trust & Safety team, to report content that violates Discord's rules, or
  • when necessary to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it

  • Confessions and replies you delete are removed from the channel right away. The record (text, author and time) is kept in the database, and the staff log keeps its copy, so moderators can still act on reports and abuse. See the next section to have this data erased.
  • Messages in a server's log channel belong to that server. The bot does not delete them; the server's staff can.
  • When a server removes the bot, all of that server's data (settings, confessions, replies, approval records and bans) is permanently deleted 30 days later. If the bot is re-added within those 30 days, the data is kept.
  • Notification preferences are kept until you change them.
  • Technical logs (such as errors) may contain server IDs. They are rotated automatically, so older entries are overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Post with or without your name. You choose each time you confess.
  • Delete your posts. Use /my-confessions, or right-click your confession or reply → Apps → Delete My Post.
  • Withdraw a confession that is still waiting for approval, from /my-confessions.
  • Control DMs. Turn reply notifications on or off with the 🔔/🔕 buttons in the bot's DMs and on your reply confirmations.
  • Erase or get a copy of your data. Contact us (see below) with your Discord user ID. We will delete your stored confessions, replies and preferences, or send you a copy, within 30 days. We cannot delete messages in a server's log channel; ask that server's staff.

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have more rights under local law, such as the right to object to processing or to complain to a data protection authority. We will honor valid requests as the law requires.

Where the data is stored

The bot and its database run on servers rented from a cloud hosting provider (DigitalOcean). These servers may be in a different country from yours. The database is not reachable from the internet; only the bot can connect to it.

No system is perfectly secure. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

You must meet Discord's minimum age requirement (at least 13, or older in some countries) to use Discord and this bot. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone below that age. If you believe a child has used the bot, contact us and we will delete their data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the bot changes. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continuing to use the bot after an update means you accept the new version.
